Preparation Guide

Accurate results depend on proper preparation. Please follow these clinical protocols carefully prior to your appointment.

01

Fast Protocol

Patients must fast (no food or liquids) for at least 8-12 hours prior to Endoscopy or Colonoscopy procedures.

02

Bowel Cleansing

A specific laxative regimen will be provided upon booking. Complete the entire preparation to ensure clear visualization.

03

Medication Review

Inform our staff of any blood thinners or diabetic medications you are currently taking at least 5 days before the procedure.
